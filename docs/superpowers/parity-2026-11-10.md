the R720 iDRAC) and netview from `feature/netview-parity` (`netview`, port 8766, data dir
`network/netview-dev`). Each bullet of spec §10 was walked on both. "Match" means the same
behaviour and the same text, adapted to the copy rules (sentence case, no middle dots).

## Fixed during the check

- The Vendor column lacked the TTL operating system hint (7674b84).
- Plugin settings labels lowercased acronyms ("Dns servers", "Ssdp timeout") and the Starlink
  router field did not say that blank means the gateway (2935991).
- On narrow charts the time-axis labels overlapped; they now drop the middle label, then the
  first, instead (e3bddcb).
- Starlink dropped clients without byte counters (33ae3ed, from the whole-branch review).

## Found, not fixed (shared with the old monitor)

- When the mesh router answers on two addresses (192.168.1.125 and .211), every sweep logs two
  "moved" events, so they fill the Events list (291 of the last 300). The old monitor does the
  same. This is a discovery rule from plan 1 and needs its own change.
