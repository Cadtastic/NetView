Plugins: Installed and Available (v10: solid tooltip, normal cursor)

Hover a dot to see its status. A rough mockup in the Graphite theme. The per-plugin entries (Health, Identity, …) are gone from the left menu. Plugins expands into Installed and Available. The plugins on the Available page are examples, not a real list.

1. Installed

Each installed plugin, built-in and folder, gets a card. Clicking a card opens its settings in an overlay (shot 2). The on/off switch stays on the card so you don't have to open the plugin to toggle it.
